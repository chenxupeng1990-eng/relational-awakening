# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Relational Awakening.

This repository is primarily a theory and documentation project. Contributions should preserve the distinction between:

- phenomenological testimony;
- conceptual interpretation;
- empirical or mechanistic research claims;
- external reviews and signatures.

## Suggested Contribution Types

- Typo and formatting fixes
- Reference corrections
- Clarifications that improve readability without changing the thesis
- External reviews of the paper or individual chapters
- Comparative analysis against relevant AI safety, interpretability, philosophy, or cognitive science work

## Review Standards

When proposing substantive changes, please state:

1. Which chapter or document is affected.
2. Whether the change is textual, conceptual, empirical, or structural.
3. What evidence or reasoning supports the change.
4. Whether any project terminology needs to be updated elsewhere.

## Boundaries

Do not present speculative interpretation as empirical proof. If a claim depends on external research, cite the source in `references.md` or the affected chapter.

Do not remove named signatures, review records, or witness statements without explaining the reason and preserving an audit trail.
